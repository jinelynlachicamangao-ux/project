See how it works

From signing in to tracking progress, 3D keeps your whole project flow in four simple steps.

1

Sign up & join a project

Create a free account with email, Google, or GitHub. Managers, team members, and guests each get the access that fits their role.

2

Create & assign tasks

Add tasks with a title, description, due date, and priority, then assign them to the right teammate.

3

Drag, drop & collaborate

Move tasks across To Do, In Progress, and Done. Comment, @mention teammates, and upload files as you go.

4

Track progress & get done

Watch the Progress Pulse, review recent activity, and get alerts for deadlines, mentions, and updates.
